# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Creative technologists, art directors, hiring managers, and fellow developers who appreciate craft. They arrive curious — expecting to see something made, not just described. They're scrolling on laptops and phones, often between meetings, looking for proof that this person can build things that feel different.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio that is itself the strongest project. The site demonstrates Three.js and WebGL mastery not by listing skills, but by being the proof — a paper-art 3D world you can explore. Success = visitors remember the experience and reach out.

## Brand Personality

Warm, organic, curious. Like a hand-made paper diorama that invites you to look closer. Not cold or technical. Not slick or corporate. The feeling of craft — something shaped by hands, not generated by templates.

## Anti-references

- Generic dark-mode developer portfolios with terminal aesthetics and monospace fonts
- SaaS landing pages with gradient heroes, metric counters, and CTA buttons
- Corporate minimalism: sterile white space, Inter font, safe grids
- Template card grids with icons above headings

## Design Principles

1. **The medium is the message** — The 3D paper-art scene IS the portfolio piece. Don't explain craft; demonstrate it.
2. **Warmth over coolness** — Paper textures, soft edges, organic shapes. Not glass, not chrome, not neon.
3. **Curiosity rewards depth** — The homepage invites exploration. Scroll, drag, look around. Every interaction should feel like turning a page.
4. **Restraint with intent** — Empty space is breathing room, not laziness. Every element earns its place.
5. **Hand-made, not hand-wavy** — Imperfect outlines, visible texture, tactile quality. The paper art style means something was crafted, not just rendered.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- Reduced motion: 3D scene should still be visible but static; scroll animations become instant transitions
- Keyboard navigable: all interactive elements reachable without mouse
- Sufficient contrast on all text, including text overlaid on the 3D scene
- Touch-friendly: 3D interactions work on mobile with touch gestures
